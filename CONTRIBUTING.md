# Contributing

Thank you for wanting to help. This project has no open licence for now: the owners keep all rights and may later offer it under any terms, including commercial ones. Contributions are accepted only from people the owners have approved, so please open an issue and ask before you send code.

## How to contribute

1. Open an issue that says what you want to change and why. Wait for a reply.
2. Once approved, send your change as a pull request. Keep it small and focused, and add or update tests.
3. Do not include personal documents, keys, passcodes or anything from your own `workspace/` folder.

- Your contribution is provided as it is, without warranty.

If you cannot agree to this, please do not open a pull request.

Approved contributors are listed by name in `CONTRIBUTORS.md`, and the git history keeps your authorship.
